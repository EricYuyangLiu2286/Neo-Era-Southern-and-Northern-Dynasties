[h2]v0.2.0 Beta update (20261114, second)[/h2]
[b]Focus:[/b] the Chinese Empire now gets ninety days' warning of republican and Yan risings, Denglai's Yan restoration works state by state through the old garrisons, the United Federation of Ming can recognise the Japanese during a civil war, and Denglai's funding no longer piles up radicals in the north without limit.

[b]What's new[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Chinese Empire · The Republican Rising (new journal):[/b] when confidence falls below 550, the court is warned of a coming republican rising and told which states will rise. If confidence is still 500 or lower ninety days later, the republicans take up arms in those states. If confidence recovers above 575, the warning lapses and no new warning comes for three months.
[*][b]Chinese Empire · The Former Yan Garrisons (new journal):[/b] once Denglai and the northeastern garrisons agree on a rising, the court is warned ninety days ahead, with the same list of states and the same date that Denglai sees. Where the republicans and the Yan contend for the same states, whichever side controls or announces them first takes precedence.
[*][b]The Road to the Restoration of Yan · state-by-state contacts:[/b] Denglai starts with bases in Songjiang and Vladivostok. Each of the other five states has its own "Contact the former garrison" button, costing 50 Yan Banner, with at least a month between contacts. The chance of success depends on the Empire's nationality law and police institution level. A successful contact secures the state and sends the Chinese Empire one of the northeastern reports. With all five states secured, the Yan Banner at 95 and the Empire in turmoil, the Articles of the Eight Provinces can be agreed, and the rising follows ninety days later once the banner reaches 100.
[*][b]United Federation of Ming · Recognise the Japanese as a Constituent People (new decision):[/b] when a secessionist, republican or socialist movement is at war with the government, and the court still owns and controls at least one incorporated state on the Japanese home islands, Japanese can be made a primary culture. Karafuto and the Ryukyus do not count, and the decision is unavailable while a Japanese separatist movement is mobilising.
[*][b]Support the Northern Republicans:[/b] each grant now adds 5% radicals at the outset and again after six months, and no longer stacks without limit. Once a year's funding ends, another six months must pass before a new grant. The button is unavailable while Denglai is a subject or ally of the Empire, and an AI Denglai stays hostile to the Empire during Across the Sea. The terms now show the weekly cost.
[*][b]Make Concessions:[/b] the button now stays visible throughout the Land Rights Revolution. Without a favourite, with a favourite outside the three eligible groups, or with confidence already full, it is greyed out and lists the reasons, naming your three groups and your current favourite.
[*][b]Character events:[/b] Duan Jingchuan's events no longer pop up repeatedly or charge twice. Mu Muzi's "Muzi Crosses the Eastern Sea" now requires her to still be in the Chinese Empire and another country to hold California.
[*][b]Names and glossary:[/b] new glossary entry "Eight Provinces". The state of Northern Heilongjiang is renamed Wuguo.
[*][b]Compatibility:[/b] the AI triggers no longer overwrite the base game file as a whole. Only the four that differ are replaced, so the rest follows base game updates.
[/list]

[b]Note:[/b] all of these changes have been fully tested; some need a new game to show up. Bug reports are welcome.
