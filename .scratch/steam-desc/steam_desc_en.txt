[h2]Mod Introduction[/h2]
This is a Chinese alternate-history mod centered on the aftermath of the “Northern Ming Warring States Era.”

After the Battle of Xiangyang, the Southern Ming Federation and the Chinese Empire stand opposed across the river. Former Ming nobles, civic republican factions, military merit elites, and the centralized Southern Court all contend with one another, as a new age—one that has neither completed unification nor decided its own destiny—unfolds across China and the wider world.

[h2]Current Content[/h2]
At present, the mod is mainly focused on the situation within China itself, with particular emphasis on the long aftermath of Northern Ming collapse and the political, military, and ideological conflict between the two great northern and southern regimes.

The main content currently implemented or in active development includes:
- The Chinese Empire route, including the Land Revolution, domestic consolidation, and the reform narrative of the Northern Court
- The Ming Federation route, including the political evolution of the Southern Court, the Wu–Ming ideological struggle, and the civil war path (if the war is mishandled, both the regional warlords and Great Wu could be wiped out by mechanics, so be careful)
- The historical continuation and character fates of the major powers shaped by the legacy of the Northern Ming Warring States Era
- Selected worldbuilding texts and national flavor content centered on China

[h2]Final Words[/h2]
This mod is still being actively expanded and completed.
Many mechanics, texts, and country-specific contents will continue to be revised and added.

If you enjoy Chinese alternate history, long-form grand strategy storytelling, and a narrative that carries the world of the “Northern Ming Warring States Era” all the way into the modern age, then you are very welcome to give it a try.
