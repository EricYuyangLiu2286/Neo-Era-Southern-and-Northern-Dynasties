[h2]v0.2.0 Beta update (20261114)[/h2]
[b]Focus:[/b] the Chinese Empire's "The Imperial People’s Confidence" now settles monthly, and reform funding is allowed while in debt. The Chinese Empire can now pick up signs of Yan activity in the northeast.

[b]What's new[/b]
[list]
[*][b]The Imperial People’s Confidence · monthly settlement:[/b] every item keeps its yearly amount but is now settled one twelfth at a time each month. Hovering over the confidence bar lists each monthly change, and unmet items show an empty box.
[*][b]The Imperial People’s Confidence · three new items:[/b] a positive budget balance gives +30 a year. A standard of living of at least 11 among labourers in the reform regions gives +30 a year. Landowners in government give −20 a year.
[*][b]Funding:[/b] "Fund Public Workshops", "Fund Transitional Relief" and their approval events no longer require enough money in the treasury. Funding is allowed while debt is at most 25% of the credit limit. "Review Land Disputes" charges a court fee and still requires a treasury balance.
[*][b]Glossary · The Imperial People’s Confidence:[/b] a new "What moves confidence" link beside the confidence bar explains which interest groups raise or lower confidence when they enter government.
[*][b]Reports from the northeast (Chinese Empire):[/b] once Denglai's preparations to restore Yan have gone far enough, the Chinese Empire will receive five routine reports from its northeastern officials. They never say it outright, but a careful reader may notice what lies behind them.
[*][b]Glossary · Zhang Baili's Northern Expedition:[/b] a new entry on the Chinese Empire's 1823–1824 campaign that destroyed Eastern Yan and swept the northeast, shown on hover in the related event.
[/list]

[b]Note:[/b] all of these changes have been fully tested and take effect when you load a save. Bug reports are welcome.
