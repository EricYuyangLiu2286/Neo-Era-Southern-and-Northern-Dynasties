[h2]v0.3.0 ‘Huangpu River’ Beta update (20261118 ※change to release date)[/h2]
[b]Focus:[/b] a navigable Yangtze, a rebuilt southern succession, the southern front and foreign intervention in the Second Jingnan War, Western Qin's frontier, a reworked Polish Problem, and national companies.

[b]What's new[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Navigable Yangtze:[/b] fleets can sail upriver through the Nanjing and Wuchang Straits, and the banks are now ten single-bank states.
[*][b]Southern succession:[/b] each of Wu's subjects has a Political Balance and takes its side when Succession Pressure reaches 100, unless Support for the Settlement fills first and settles it without war.
[*][b]Living Under the Rites:[/b] now has civil war, Zheng peace and Ming peace versions, and the civil war winner gets its own hierarchy and four laws.
[*][b]Second Jingnan War:[/b] Western Qin and Dongzhou can send troops against Wu, Wu's southern attacks face Fever and Mountain Roads, and Great Wu gets an Army Morale bar.
[*][b]Japan:[/b] a Japan that turns on Wu fights its own war, so the mainland settles without waiting.
[*][b]Western Qin:[/b] new journal The Frontier Peoples, where Afghan and steppe separatists really do rise; The Frontier Uprising is removed.
[*][b]Persia:[/b] its course is now set by Which Way the Generalship Turns.
[*][b]The Ruler and the Cabinet (new journal):[/b] non-republican empires that cannot appoint favourites can appoint a prime minister.
[*][b]The Polish Problem:[/b] reworked with the Reds and the Whites, crisis windows, the Continental Congress and the Polish Pilgrims, and settled by 1860.
[*][b]National companies:[/b] 19 new companies for the mod's countries, each with its own prestige good.
[*][b]Fixes:[/b] seven periodic checks that never ran now run monthly, plus various error fixes.
[/list]

[b]Note:[/b] all of these changes have been fully tested. Saves from 0.2.0 cannot be continued; please start a new game. Bug reports are welcome.
