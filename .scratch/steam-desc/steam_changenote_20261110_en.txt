[h2]v0.2.0 Beta update (20261110)[/h2]
[b]Focus:[/b] the failed ending of the Chinese Empire's Land Rights Revolution and the republican uprising, Western Qin's starting institutions, and the aftermath of the War to Opium.

[b]What's new[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Chinese Empire · military estates:[/b] if the Land Rights Revolution fails, the government concludes the Twenty-Year Military Estates Settlement with the army and the landowners. Tenant Farmers carries the Military Estates Ownership Act and Professional Army carries Chartered Military Districts, both backed by the Armed Forces and the Landowners. For 20 years neither the land reform law nor the army model law can be changed, and neither amendment can be repealed.
[*][b]Western Qin:[/b] starts in 1836 with the Military Estates Ownership Act and Chartered Military Districts. Other countries can propose the two amendments when their Landowners (or Armed Forces) are in government or powerful, or when their primary culture belongs to the Sinosphere. The Chinese Empire still needs the twenty-year settlement first.
[*][b]Land Rights Revolution:[/b] the "Review Land Disputes" button now lists each requirement, with the fee, thresholds and the 12-month term. "Suspended Land Clearance" is now −10% prestige, −5% authority and +15% political movement attraction for ten years. Land crisis events and modifiers were reorganised; two old events that could never fire and four unused modifiers were removed.
[*][b]Republican uprising:[/b] the Empire's war goal against the rebels is now "Suppress the Uprising": victory annexes the republic and, if the Land Rights Revolution is still under way, adds +15 reform progress and −20 disorder. The rebels fight for "Humiliation (Republican Uprising)": victory leaves the Empire "Humbled by the Republicans" for ten years, with −10% army offense and defense and +15% political movement radicalism from opposed enactments.
[*][b]War to Opium:[/b] only a country that actually pushed opium on you can become the target of an opium ban war; the reversed victory and defeat hints are corrected.
[*][b]Overcoming Opium Addiction (new journal):[/b] a country suffering from the Opium Epidemic can issue five-year Opium Control Regulations. Addiction falls faster with an opium ban, with health or police institutions at level 2, or when the average standard of living of your primary cultures is above 10; below 8 it climbs back. Once addiction drops to 40% or below, the Opium Epidemic is lifted. While the regulations are in force, the Petty Bourgeoisie, Landowners and Industrialists each lose 3 approval.
[/list]

[b]Note:[/b] all of these changes have been fully tested; some need a new game to show up. Bug reports are welcome.
