[h2]v0.2.0 Beta update (20261107, since the 20261030 original)[/h2]
[quote]
[b]Please read first:[/b]
[list]
[*]This version is still a beta. This round changed a great deal and leaned heavily on vibe coding, so it may be less stable than earlier versions.
[*]The update was made with the help of GPT Astra and Claude Opus. Skill development, direction, debugging and testing were done by me. That said, I can't guarantee everything is playable yet; much of the new content has only been checked outside the game.
[*]So all kinds of comments and bug reports are welcome. If you don't like vibe coding, feel free to say so too, and if it isn't for you, you can unsubscribe for now and come back for a more stable version later.

[b]Focus:[/b] main updates to Western Qin, Japan and Dongzhou; the Ming Federation, Chinese Empire and France reworked; opening journals and event networks filled in for Europe, North America, the Middle East, India and the Pacific.

[b]What's new[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Victoria 3 1.15.0:[/b] all journals, outcomes and progress bars moved to the new interfaces; frequent new-version errors fixed. The Anglo-Russian Great Game is switched off; Russia's frontier route stays, aligned with Western Qin.
[*][b]Chinese Empire:[/b] the Land Revolution is split into workable sub-journals (land commodification, urbanisation, estate reform), with public confidence, a republican revolt and a cost for stalling reform.
[*][b]Ming Federation / Wu:[/b] southern factions and crisis timing reworked; fixes to the Jingnan holdouts, Huai and Philippine aftermaths, the Ming loyalist settlement and Ming succession; HNA now joins the Ming side when war opens; USM monthly events restored.
[*][b]Western Qin and Persia:[/b] Qin state-building and rule agendas, unity and breakup, the throne vs. regions vs. army, recovering old lands; Persian governance with claims you build up and spend; Russia's frontier route.
[*][b]Japan:[/b] development journals, the three-way partition and unity, cultural drift under low unity, policy choices in the political struggle; "Across the Strait" now offers governor-general and council routes.
[*][b]India and the South Seas:[/b] staged development, princely-state diplomacy and regional uprisings; hold 8 Indian regions to found the Tianzhu Pacification Commission (a dominion), plus the Yuanshan Pacification Commission; France gains direct rule over Gujarat and nearby lands with a local squadron.
[*][b]Dongzhou (HUS):[/b] three registration laws and social hierarchies, Manchu-Mongol and native routes, the Native Khanate and its war for survival, road projects; Aixue split into Yujing, Shuohai and Linmang Aixue.
[*][b]United States:[/b] the slavery question rewritten with a Crisis of the Union bar. The Civil War usually breaks out in 1860–1862 and no later than 1872; reconciliation is possible but hard. Dongzhou gets "The Western Counterweight": shelter escapees, build claims, push east.
[*][b]Elsewhere:[/b] French empire and dynasty chain repaired; Ottoman expansion and army reform, Egyptian investment and independence, Prussia's common German defence, Austria and Italy, Mexico and Peru, Central American autonomy; a Korean restoration movement after Korea falls; Polish events fixed.
[*][b]Natives and diaspora:[/b] native restoration wars; after a failed uprising, people leave in batches with the destination state shown in advance.
[*][b]Interface and text:[/b] large review and rewrite of Chinese and English text; 224 missing modifier icons added; diplomatic request buttons no longer always say "never"; every journal shows how it ends, and progress bars list each source on hover.
[*][b]Map:[/b] finer state splits around Dzungaria, Tianshan, Ili, Gansu, Fujian and Guangzhou, and northern California (North and South Dayuan).
[/list]

[b]Not done yet:[/b] mid- and late-game content is still thin; lore-accurate South and Latin American content is not in yet; France's new successor routes (such as "Court and Provinces"), more distinct political choices in Japan's reforms, new art (portraits, scenes, main menu).
