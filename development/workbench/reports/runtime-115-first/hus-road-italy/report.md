# Dongqing road projects, Italian Common Undertaking, and southern wording

### Road projects
The existing HUS chapter is now a pinned selector. Chartered contractors, public tenders and locally financed routes each attach a distinct pinned journal with its own bilingual title, operating-month bar, checkable requirements, selected-state name, levels, market access and consecutive months. A selected charter still requires profitable, 75%-staffed industrial capacity to reach baseline plus 20%, rounded up and clamped to an increment of 2–8. Existing rail capacity satisfies a tender and existing ports or railways satisfy local finance if at least one level is 50%-staffed. All still require 80% market access, a serviceable freight link, solvent finances and no local war involving the funding country.

The original start, 12-month expense, 24-month local contract terms, political effects, three-month streak, award and retarget payment semantics remain authoritative. The dedicated entries never charge or settle. A valid saved project attaches without resetting its recorded baseline or streak. After payment expiry, use Change the Plan or Project Site and select the new contract on the chapter. An old child invalidates if its route has changed; the next selected child attaches after that instance is gone, avoiding concurrent pinned details. Temporary absence during native JE reevaluation is possible. The parent remains usable. No pre-emptive data reconstruction for damaged saves.

Candidate states must have or be able to build the relevant industries and transport. The AI can place one ordinary state-scoped construction order into an empty public queue if the required physical levels are lacking, finances permit and the site is free of construction; it earns no instant building, employment, profit or project progress. It will not retarget a project already meeting its requirements or building at the site. Native precedents are land_ownership_law_events.txt:1773 and british_dictates.txt for state-scoped construction and eligibility. Actual 1.15 dump entries include effects.log:33850, triggers.log:6184, 6543 and 8961.

### Italy
Reviewed all nineteen buttons on the persistent journal. Seven billetateral proposals and termination previously hid their entire payload. They now explain the actual exchange and its timing; neither a proposal nor a counteroffer claims automatic acceptance. A valid, mutual agreement shows its actual partner and local streak. A broken agreement can release the local obligation without counterparty mutation. One-time direction choices and domestic starts disappear after selection. Unavailable funding sites get specific feedback. Once foundations are complete the journal distinguishes available partners from having no eligible counterpart.

The site validator previously accepted any still-existing state, irrespective of a transfer or depopulation. Both countries must now have a valid local state before accepting and paying. Preparing an inactive state refreshes a stale reference and asking a new partner prepares both sides. Existing paid contracts, original answer windows, alternatives, the 12-month treasury cost and cooldown, and each party's right to refuse are preserved. Six domestic selectors likewise invalidate recorded sites no longer satisfying their real occupancy and ownership eligibility.

### Southern campaign

This follows the new regional opposition predicate, including republican participants. The two South files have a separate updated manifest under save-war-audit/ui.
